# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
OpenSync is for people who move between devices and want their content to follow them without friction. The earliest core audience is technical users, especially developers and other tech-forward people who value speed, clarity, and tools that stay out of the way. Over time, the product should also serve broader individual and team use cases, from clipboard sync today to files and other shareable content later.

## Product Purpose
OpenSync exists to make your useful content feel present wherever you are. It starts with clipboard syncing across your own devices, then expands into a wider sync layer for files and other content, with the same promise intact: what you need should be available when you need it, without a bloated or confusing workflow. Success means the product feels simpler and more direct than traditional cloud storage or sharing tools, while still feeling dependable and modern.

## Brand Personality
Calm and effortless, with a technically fluent voice. The brand should feel clear, capable, and quietly confident rather than loud, overexplained, or overly clever. Marketing should make syncing feel lightweight and reassuring, not like infrastructure the user has to manage.

## Anti-references
Avoid interfaces that feel overwhelming, crowded, or operationally heavy. Do not drift toward the visual or interaction patterns of complex cloud suites that foreground management, configuration, or feature sprawl. Avoid noisy dashboards, dense enterprise styling, and any marketing language that makes a simple idea feel complicated.

The positive reference direction is the compositional confidence and atmosphere of the Hazyhues reference, combined with the product clarity and technical sharpness associated with Raycast. The goal is not to imitate either one directly, but to combine emotional calm with precise utility.

## Design Principles
1. Simplicity should feel like the product's strongest feature, not the absence of features.
2. Show continuity across devices and contexts, so the product always communicates presence and availability.
3. Speak to technical users with confidence and clarity, without becoming niche, cold, or exclusionary.
4. Keep interactions lightweight, obvious, and low-friction across both the landing page and desktop app.
5. As the sync surface expands from clipboard to files and beyond, preserve the same calm mental model instead of turning growth into complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA as the default accessibility bar. Support clear contrast, robust keyboard access, and readable hierarchy across marketing and product surfaces. Motion can be expressive where it adds value, but it should remain purposeful and avoid undermining clarity or comfort. Reduced-motion handling should be preserved when richer animation is introduced.
